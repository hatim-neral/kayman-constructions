Covered Patios and Outdoor Kitchens in Houston

An outdoor room in Houston needs shade, a fan and a roof that stays put in a storm. We build covered patios that tie into your roofline properly and outdoor kitchens with real gas and power.

Covered patio in The Woodlands

What’s included

  • Covered patios tied into the roof
  • Pergolas (wood or aluminum)
  • Outdoor kitchens and grills
  • Gas, water and electrical runs
  • Ceiling fans and lighting
  • Concrete and paver patios
  • Outdoor fireplaces
  • Screened porches

How long it takes

Covered patios take 3 to 6 weeks. Adding an outdoor kitchen usually adds 1 to 2 weeks.

Built for Houston houses

Wind load

Patio covers act like sails in a storm. We engineer the posts, footings and connections to current wind-load requirements.

Drainage and slope

A new slab or pavers change how water leaves the yard. We slope everything away from the house and tie into existing drainage.

Questions

Does a patio cover need a permit?

In most Houston-area cities, yes, and HOAs often need to approve it too. We handle both.

Will a patio cover make my living room dark?

It can. We use skylights, taller ceilings or a pergola section to keep light coming into the house.
