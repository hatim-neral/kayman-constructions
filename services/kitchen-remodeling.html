Kitchen Remodeling in Houston

A kitchen remodel is mostly about what you can’t see: where the water, gas, venting and circuits run. We plan those first, then build the room around how you cook.

Kitchen remodel in Katy

What’s included

  • Layout changes and wall removal
  • Custom and semi-custom cabinetry
  • Quartz, granite and marble counters
  • Islands with seating, sinks or cooktops
  • Gas line and plumbing relocation
  • Dedicated appliance circuits
  • Range hoods vented outside
  • Tile backsplashes and flooring
  • Recessed, pendant and under-cabinet lighting

How long it takes

Most kitchen remodels take 6 to 10 weeks of construction once materials are on site. Custom cabinets add lead time, so we order them as soon as the design is signed.

Built for Houston houses

Venting that actually vents

Many Houston kitchens have recirculating hoods. We duct new hoods to the outside, which matters when the AC runs ten months a year and cooking moisture has nowhere else to go.

Slab plumbing

Most homes built since the 1960s sit on a slab, so moving a sink to an island means cutting the slab. We scope it before pricing so it isn’t a surprise mid-job.

Questions

Can we live in the house during a kitchen remodel?

Usually, yes. We seal the work area with zip walls and negative air, and we can set up a temporary kitchen with a sink and a counter in another room.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. Moving plumbing, gas or electrical needs a permit in every city we work in, and we pull and close them out ourselves.

Can we keep our existing cabinets?

Sometimes. If the boxes are solid, refacing or painting can save a lot. We’ll tell you honestly at the walkthrough.

Planning kitchen remodeling?

We’ll walk the house with you, talk through what you want and give you a line-item estimate.
