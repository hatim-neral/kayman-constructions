Wall Removal and Open-Concept Remodeling in Houston

Taking out a wall is quick. Taking out a load-bearing wall safely takes an engineer, temporary shoring, a properly sized beam and a permit. We handle all of it.

Open-concept remodel in Sugar Land

What’s included

  • Structural engineering
  • Temporary shoring
  • Flush or exposed beams (wood, LVL or steel)
  • Rerouting ducts, pipes and wiring in the wall
  • Floor patching and refinishing
  • Ceiling repair and new lighting
  • Fireplace and built-in updates

How long it takes

Removing a wall and installing a beam takes 1 to 3 weeks. Most clients pair it with a kitchen remodel, which sets the overall schedule.

Built for Houston houses

Ducts in the way

Houston homes run a lot of ductwork through interior walls and attics. We plan where the returns and supplies go before the wall comes down, not after.

Flush beams

Most people want the beam hidden in the ceiling. In a single-story house that usually works; in a two-story it depends on the floor framing above.

Questions

How do you know if a wall is load-bearing?

We check the attic and the framing, and a structural engineer confirms it and sizes the beam.

Will there be a post in the middle?

Not usually. Longer spans need a bigger beam, but posts can almost always be hidden in the remaining walls.

Planning open-concept remodeling?
