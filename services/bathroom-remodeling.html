Bathroom Remodeling in Houston

Most failed bathrooms fail behind the tile. We tear down to the studs or the slab, waterproof the whole wet area, and then build the room you picked out.

Bathroom remodel in The Heights

What’s included

  • Tub-to-shower conversions
  • Curbless and walk-in showers
  • Freestanding tubs
  • Double vanities and makeup counters
  • Full waterproofing membranes
  • Heated floors
  • Frameless glass
  • Exhaust fans ducted outside
  • Grab bars and aging-in-place layouts

How long it takes

A primary bath usually takes 4 to 7 weeks of construction. A hall bath with a simple layout can be done in about 3.

Built for Houston houses

Humidity and mold

We use cement board and a continuous waterproofing membrane in wet areas, and fans sized for the room and ducted outside, not into the attic.

Pier-and-beam houses

In older homes on piers, a curbless shower means lowering the floor framing. We check the joists before promising a zero-threshold entry.

Questions

Can you convert a tub to a walk-in shower?

Yes, it’s one of our most common jobs. The drain usually has to move and the floor has to slope, which we handle as part of the remodel.

How do you prevent leaks?

A continuous membrane under the tile, flood-tested before tile goes on. We photograph it so you have a record.

Planning bathroom remodeling?

We’ll walk the house with you, talk through what you want and give you a line-item estimate.
