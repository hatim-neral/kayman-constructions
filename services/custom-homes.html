Custom Home Building in Houston

Whether it’s a teardown in Bellaire or an empty lot in The Woodlands, we build custom homes with the same crew and the same project lead from foundation to final walkthrough.

What’s included

  • Lot evaluation and surveys
  • Working with your architect, or ours
  • Engineered foundations
  • Framing, roofing and envelope
  • Energy-efficient mechanical systems
  • Interior finishes and millwork
  • Floodplain elevation where required
  • Permits and final inspections

How long it takes

A custom home typically takes 10 to 16 months of construction after permits are issued.

Built for Houston houses

Floodplain rules

Many Houston-area lots now require the finished floor to sit above the 500-year floodplain. We check the survey before design so the house works the first time.

Energy code

We build tight, insulated houses with properly sized HVAC, which is what keeps a Houston summer bill reasonable.

Where we do this

  • Custom homes in The Woodlands
  • Custom homes in Bellaire
  • Custom homes in Memorial

We also work in The Heights, Katy, Sugar Land, Pearland, Kingwood and across Greater Houston.

Questions

Do you build on my lot?

Yes, we build on lots our clients own. We can also help you evaluate a lot before you buy it.

Do I need an architect?

You can bring your own, or we can introduce you to architects we work with.

Planning custom homes?

We’ll walk the house with you, talk through what you want and give you a line-item estimate.
