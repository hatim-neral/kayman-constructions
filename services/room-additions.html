How long it takes

Most additions take 4 to 8 months from permit to move-in. Permits and HOA review can add 4 to 10 weeks up front, depending on the city.

Built for Houston houses

Tying into the slab

A new foundation has to move with the old one without cracking where they meet. We use engineered piers and doweled connections designed for expansive clay.

Drainage

An addition covers ground that used to absorb rain. We regrade and add gutters and drains so water moves away from both the new and old foundations.

Questions

Do I need a permit for an addition?

Always. We prepare the drawings, get engineering and pull the permit.

Is it better to build up or out?

It depends on the lot, setbacks and the existing foundation. We look at both at the walkthrough.
