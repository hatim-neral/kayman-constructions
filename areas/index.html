Where we work

Greater Houston, from inside the 610 Loop out past the Grand Parkway. Each area has its own permits, HOAs and housing stock, and we plan around them.

  • The HeightsOne of Houston’s oldest neighborhoods, founded in 1891, full of bungalows and Victorians that need careful work.
  • KatyMaster-planned communities west of Houston, where most homes are 10 to 30 years old and ready for their first real update.
  • Sugar LandEstablished neighborhoods from the 1980s and 1990s, where formal living rooms and closed kitchens are ready to come down.
  • The WoodlandsWooded lots and village neighborhoods north of Houston, built from the 1970s on.
  • PearlandOne of the fastest-growing cities in the region, where families are adding space instead of moving.
  • BellaireA small city inside Houston, where 1950s ranch homes sit next to new two-stories.
  • MemorialLarge wooded lots and mid-century homes in west Houston and the Memorial Villages.
  • KingwoodThe “Livable Forest” in northeast Houston, with homes from the 1970s through the 1990s.

Don’t see your neighborhood? We work across Greater Houston, so ask anyway.
