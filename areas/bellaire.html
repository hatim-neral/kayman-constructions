Remodeling and Renovation in Bellaire, TX

A small city inside Houston, where 1950s ranch homes sit next to new two-stories.

Homes in Bellaire

Bellaire was largely built out with 1950s ranch houses, and many have since been replaced with new homes. The ranch houses that remain are often worth renovating for their lot and location.

Permits and approvals

Bellaire is its own city with its own building department, separate from the City of Houston. Floodplain rules require elevated finished floors for new homes and substantial renovations in the floodplain.

Soil, trees and flooding

Parts of Bellaire flooded during Tropical Storm Allison in 2001 and Hurricane Harvey in 2017. We check whether a renovation crosses the substantial-improvement threshold that triggers elevation requirements.
