Remodeling and Renovation in Katy, TX

Master-planned communities west of Houston, where most homes are 10 to 30 years old and ready for their first real update.

Homes in Katy

Much of Katy was built from the 1990s on, in communities like Cinco Ranch and Cross Creek Ranch. The houses are solid, but many still have builder-grade kitchens and baths and closed-off formal rooms that owners want opened up.

Permits and approvals

Homes inside Katy city limits are permitted by the City of Katy. Most master-planned communities sit in unincorporated county areas, where the HOA’s architectural review is the main approval for exterior work. We submit to both.

Soil, trees and flooding

Homes near the Addicks and Barker reservoirs flooded during Hurricane Harvey in 2017. We check floodplain maps before planning additions or new slabs.
