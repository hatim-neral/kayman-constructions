Kitchen remodel in Katy

A 1990s builder kitchen with oak cabinets and a checkered floor, turned into a bright kitchen with a marble island that seats five.

Sample project. The photos are stock images showing the kind of work described, not this specific house. Replace them with real Kayman project photos before launch.

The house

A 1998 two-story in Cinco Ranch, bought by a young family in 2019. The kitchen was the original builder package: orange oak cabinets, a soffit above the uppers, a laminate counter and a small peninsula that cut the room off from the breakfast nook.

Why they called us

Both parents cook, usually at the same time, with two kids doing homework at the counter. The peninsula forced everyone into one narrow aisle, and the microwave hood just pushed steam back into the room. They had collected three quotes for new cabinets, but nobody had asked how they actually used the kitchen.

The plan

At the walkthrough we watched how traffic moved from the garage door to the fridge and the breakfast table. The plan removed the peninsula and the soffits, moved the range to the outside wall so a real hood could vent outdoors, and put a nine-foot marble island in the middle with seating on two sides. Cabinets go to the ceiling, so the soffit space became storage.

What came up

Moving the range meant running a new gas line through the slab edge and the exterior wall. Behind the old soffit we found a bathroom drain line from upstairs, so we rerouted it into the wall cavity instead of boxing it back in. Cabinets were ordered the day the design was signed, and demolition started when they were two weeks out, so the kitchen was never open longer than it had to be.

The result

Nine weeks of construction, with a temporary kitchen set up in the laundry room. Two people can cook side by side, the kids have their own side of the island, and the hood vents outside instead of into the ceiling.
