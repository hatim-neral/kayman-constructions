Primary suite addition in Pearland

A 480 sq ft primary suite added to the side of a 2008 home, with its own bath and closet.

Sample project. The photos are stock images showing the kind of work described, not this specific house. Replace them with real Kayman project photos before launch.

The house

A 2008 one-story in Shadow Creek Ranch: four bedrooms, a family of five, and a primary bedroom that shared a wall with the kids’ rooms. The side yard was wide, unused and mostly weeds.

Why they called us

They had priced moving and decided they liked the neighborhood, the schools and the commute too much to leave. What they needed was a quiet room of their own, with a bath and a real closet.

The plan

A 480 sq ft suite on the side of the house, with a bedroom, a bath and a walk-in closet. The new foundation sits on engineered piers, doweled into the existing slab so the two move together. The hip roof, framing and brick all match the original, and the suite gets its own HVAC zone so it doesn’t over-cool the rest of the house.

What came up

HOA review and the City of Pearland permit took seven weeks together, mostly waiting on the HOA’s monthly meeting. We tracked down the brick plant that made the original brick, so the new wall matches without a visible seam.

The result

Five months of construction, with the family living at home throughout. From the street, the addition looks like it was always part of the house.
