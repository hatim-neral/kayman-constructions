Covered patio in The Woodlands

A bare concrete slab turned into a shaded outdoor room with a pergola, lighting and planting.

Sample project. The photos are stock images showing the kind of work described, not this specific house. Replace them with real Kayman project photos before launch.

The house

A 1994 two-story in one of The Woodlands’ older villages, backing onto tall pines. The backyard had a small concrete slab and a fence, and nowhere shaded to sit from May to October.

Why they called us

The owners wanted to eat outside most evenings, but the slab baked in the afternoon sun and the yard held water after every storm.

The plan

Extend the slab, regrade the yard so water drains away from the house, and add an aluminum louvered pergola that opens for winter sun and closes for summer shade and rain. New outlets, lighting and planting beds finish the space.

What came up

The pergola design went through The Woodlands’ residential design review before we ordered it. Two mature pines stood close to the new slab, so we placed the footings to avoid their main roots instead of removing the trees.

The result

Four weeks of construction. The yard drains after storms, the pines stayed, and the patio is shaded through the hottest part of the day.
