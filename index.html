    Built for Houston

    A house here deals with shifting clay, long summers and hurricane season. We plan for all three from the first walkthrough.

    Clay soil that moves

    Gulf Coast clay swells after rain and shrinks in drought. We check the foundation before we open a wall, and we grade and gutter so water runs away from the slab.

    Heat and humidity

    Closed-cell insulation, properly sized HVAC and mold-resistant board in wet rooms, so a new room stays comfortable in August.

    Hurricane season

    Impact-rated windows, hurricane straps and roof decking fastened to current code. While the walls are open, we can pre-wire for a standby generator.

    Floodplains and permits

    We check floodplain rules before design, not after, and pull permits with the City of Houston and surrounding cities and counties.

    How a project goes

    1. Walkthrough

      We walk your home with you, take measurements and talk through what you want and a realistic budget.

    2. Design and estimate

      You get drawings, material selections and a line-item estimate you can compare with anyone else's.

    3. Permits and schedule

      We handle permits and HOA approvals, then give you a start date and a calendar for the job.

    4. Build

      One project lead on site, a written update every week, and a swept site at the end of each day.

    5. Final walkthrough

      We walk the finished work with you, close out the punch list and hand over your warranty.

    Two partners on every job
