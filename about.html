Two partners, one standard for every house

Kayman Constructions is a Houston remodeling and building company run by its two partners, Kaif and Amaan. One of us is on every project, from the first walkthrough to the last punch-list item.

How it started

Kaif and Amaan started Kayman Constructions with a simple idea: remodeling a house should feel organized, not chaotic. Between them, they had seen how most home projects go. Estimates grow after the contract is signed, schedules slip without explanation, and when something goes wrong nobody picks up the phone. They wanted to run a company where the homeowner always knows what is happening, what it costs and who is responsible. Kayman is the result: two partners, one standard, and one of them on every job.

Why we do it this way

Most people who call a contractor have already been burned once: a vague estimate, a crew that disappears for weeks, a finish that looked nothing like the drawing. We built Kayman around the opposite. Every job gets a line-item estimate you can compare, a written schedule, and one partner who is responsible for it from start to finish. When something behind the walls changes the plan, you hear it from us that day, with the options and the cost.

Why Houston

Houston houses have their own problems: clay soil that moves, ten months of air conditioning, hurricane season and floodplain rules that change from one city to the next. Greater Houston is also one of the fastest-growing regions in the country, full of houses that need to work harder: older homes inside the Loop that deserve careful renovation, and newer suburban homes that families would rather grow into than leave. We plan for those things at the first walkthrough instead of discovering them halfway through the job.

Where we’re going

We’re growing one finished house at a time, and we would rather do fewer projects well than more projects badly. Our measure of success is simple: every client should be glad to recommend us to their neighbors, and every house we touch should be better built than the day we found it.

Build. Develop. Deliver.

What our name promises

Build.

We build to code and then past it, with the parts nobody sees (waterproofing, flashing, framing connections) done as carefully as the parts everybody does.

Develop.

We develop the plan with you before we pick up a tool: drawings, selections, permits and a schedule, so the build follows a plan you’ve already agreed to.

Deliver.

We deliver what the estimate says, on the schedule we gave you, and we walk the finished house with you until the punch list is empty.
